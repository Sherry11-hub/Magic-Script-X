TECHWAVE 2025 — SPEAKER BIOS & PROFILES
========================================

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SARAH CHEN | Opening Ceremony Host & Awards Host
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Full Name: Sarah Chen
Title: Chief Executive Officer
Organization: TechWave Foundation
Session: Opening Ceremony (09:00 AM) & Closing Panel Host (05:05 PM)

Bio:
Sarah Chen is the founding CEO of the TechWave Foundation, a non-profit 
dedicated to making technology education accessible globally. With over 
18 years in the tech industry — including leadership roles at Google and 
Microsoft — Sarah has become one of Silicon Valley's most recognized voices 
on inclusive innovation. She holds an MBA from Wharton and an undergraduate 
degree in Computer Science from MIT. In 2023, she was named one of Forbes' 
"50 Women Shaping the Future of Technology."

MC Notes: Sarah will host the Opening Ceremony and the Closing Panel. 
She is known for her warm, energetic delivery and sharp wit.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DR. MARCUS RIVERA | Keynote Speaker (Morning)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Full Name: Dr. Marcus Rivera
Title: Chief Artificial Intelligence Officer (CAIO)
Organization: NeuralPath Inc.
Session: Keynote — "The Decade of AI" (10:00 AM, Grand Auditorium)

Bio:
Dr. Marcus Rivera is a pioneering AI researcher and the Chief Artificial 
Intelligence Officer at NeuralPath Inc., a leading AI infrastructure company 
valued at $4.2 billion. He holds a PhD in Machine Learning from Carnegie 
Mellon University and has published over 60 peer-reviewed papers on 
deep learning, neural architecture, and AI safety. Before NeuralPath, 
Dr. Rivera spent a decade at DARPA leading the Advanced AI Research Group. 
His keynote, "The Decade of AI," draws on exclusive research forecasting 
the next ten years of AI development across industries.

Pronouns: He/Him
Introduction note: Dr. Rivera prefers to be introduced with his full title 
and mentions of his CMU PhD background.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PROF. AISHA OKONKWO | Panel Moderator
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Full Name: Professor Aisha Okonkwo
Title: Associate Professor of AI Ethics & Law
Organization: Stanford University, Human-Centered AI Institute
Session: Panel Moderator — "Ethics in Generative AI" (11:30 AM)

Bio:
Professor Aisha Okonkwo is a globally acclaimed scholar at the intersection 
of artificial intelligence, ethics, and public policy. At Stanford's 
Human-Centered AI Institute, she leads the Algorithmic Accountability Lab. 
Her landmark report "Whose Intelligence?" (2024) influenced AI legislation 
in both the EU and Canada. She advises the United Nations AI Advisory Body 
and was named a TIME100 AI honoree in 2024.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DR. PRIYA NAIR | Workshop Facilitator
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Full Name: Dr. Priya Nair
Title: Principal Engineer, AI Research
Organization: Anthropic
Session: Workshop — "Building RAG Systems" (02:30 PM, Grand Auditorium)

Bio:
Dr. Priya Nair is a Principal Engineer on the Applied AI team at Anthropic, 
where she specializes in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, 
constitutional AI, and enterprise LLM deployment. She has 12 years of 
industry experience and holds a PhD in Natural Language Processing from 
the University of Edinburgh. She is the co-author of the widely-cited 
paper "Production-Grade RAG: Lessons from Scale" (NeurIPS 2024). 
Dr. Nair is passionate about making advanced AI accessible to practitioners 
and runs the popular newsletter "RAG Weekly."

Session Notes: Hands-on workshop. Attendees need Python 3.10+ on their laptops.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TOM BLACKWELL | Afternoon Keynote Speaker
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Full Name: Tom Blackwell
Title: Co-Founder & Managing Partner
Organization: ChainLink Ventures
Session: Keynote — "Web3 & the Open Internet" (04:20 PM, Grand Auditorium)

Bio:
Tom Blackwell is the co-founder of ChainLink Ventures, a $350M venture fund 
focused exclusively on Web3, decentralized infrastructure, and open-source 
AI. A serial entrepreneur, Tom founded and exited two blockchain startups 
before launching ChainLink in 2021. His talk, "Web3 & the Open Internet," 
challenges the assumption that AI and decentralization are in conflict, 
arguing instead that open-weight models and decentralized compute are the 
only path to a fair AI future.
